Concepts:
Non-Equi Joins

 The joins we used so far are called equi-joins because they use the equality sign (=) in the joining condition. Non-equi are all other joins that use any other operators – comparison operators (<, >, <=, >=, !=, <>), the BETWEEN operator, or any other logical condition – to join tables.
